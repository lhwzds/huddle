- Lumen is a 14-person B2B analytics startup: customers stream event data into our ingest platform and use our dashboards.
- Mira (CFO) owns money, the forecast and the board; Theo (CTO) owns engineering and the platform; Juno (Head of Customers) owns sales and accounts.
- "Kestrel" is Theo's internal project: the rewrite of the ingest platform, in progress. Only Theo knows its real status and numbers.
- Acme Logistics is a large prospect Juno is working on. Only Juno knows the terms.
- Last quarter two customers churned and Q3 came in under plan. The details sit with whoever owns them.
- The board has rules about runway; Mira knows them.
